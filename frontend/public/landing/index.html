What CourseForge does

01

A timeline that fits your term

Give it a topic, your experience level, and the hours you can give a day. The model sizes the path to the subject’s real complexity — not a fixed twelve weeks — and lays out focus areas, practice tasks and a mini-exercise for each one.

02

Resources already gathered

Every week arrives with live video and reading links pulled in for that week’s material, so the first hour of studying is studying — not forty open tabs and a search box.

03

Milestones you have to pass

Three multiple-choice questions and two written answers close out each week. Multiple choice is scored exactly; written answers are read and marked with per-question feedback, so “I think I got it” becomes something you can check.

04

Study groups, without the comparison

Share an invite code and learn the same skill together. Your hours and your pace stay private — the group board shows completed milestones and points only. Nobody sees that you set twenty minutes a day.

05

Start from your résumé

Upload a résumé and get the gaps back as a starting point: what you already have evidence for, what the role you’re aiming at expects, and which of the two the next eight weeks should close.

Built for students first

For the sixteen weeks nobody scheduled

Coursework has a syllabus. Everything else — the internship stack, the language, the thing the job posting assumed you knew — has nothing. CourseForge is for that second list: a plan for the semester’s spare hours, honest about how few of them there are, and equally useful to anyone else learning on their own time.
